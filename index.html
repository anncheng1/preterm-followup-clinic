HINE Narrative Notes

General Movements Assessment (GMA)
Applicable from birth to 20 weeks corrected age. Absent fidgety movements are a strong predictor of cerebral palsy.
🎬

GMA Classification

0–20 weeks CA
⚠️ Absent fidgety movements (F−) at 9–20 weeks CA have ~98% sensitivity for later CP. Refer urgently to developmental paediatrics / physiotherapy.
